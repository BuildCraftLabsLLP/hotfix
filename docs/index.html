FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything people ask before installing Hotfix.

What is Hotfix?

Hotfix is a native system-tray app for macOS, Windows and Linux that monitors CPU usage and automatically terminates runaway processes before your fan spins up, your machine overheats, or your battery drains.

Is Hotfix free?

Yes. Hotfix is completely free and open source on macOS, Windows and Linux. There is no subscription, no account, and no telemetry.

Which operating systems does Hotfix support?

Hotfix runs on macOS 13 Ventura or later (Apple Silicon and Intel), on Windows 11 (64-bit), and on 64-bit Linux (Intel/AMD or ARM) with a 2022-or-newer distribution such as Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora or Linux Mint, on a desktop with a system tray.

How does Hotfix decide which processes to kill?

Hotfix checks every running process every 5 seconds. When a process stays above your chosen CPU threshold for longer than your chosen duration, Hotfix terminates it and shows a notification. You control both the threshold and the duration.

Will Hotfix kill important system processes?

No. Critical system processes such as kernel_task, WindowServer and Finder are permanently excluded and can never be terminated, and you can add your own trusted apps to a whitelist.

Does Hotfix collect or send my data?

No. Hotfix has no telemetry and no account. It runs entirely on your machine; the only network request it makes is checking GitHub for new versions so it can update itself silently.
